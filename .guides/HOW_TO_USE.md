# 🌿 How to Use Project Eirene Dashboard

## Super Simple Guide

### Step 1: Open the Dashboard
The dashboard is already running! Just open your browser and go to:
```
http://localhost:5001
```

### Step 2: Click a Community Button
You'll see 6 colorful buttons representing different online communities:

- **😰 r/anxiety** - People sharing anxiety experiences
- **😫 r/burnout** - Work exhaustion stories  
- **😔 r/lonely** - Feelings of isolation
- **😢 r/depression** - Depression discussions
- **😤 r/vent** - General stress venting
- **🐦 Twitter/X** - Mental health conversations

**Just click any button!** That's it!

### Step 3: Watch the Magic Happen
After clicking, you'll see:

1. **Loading Animation** - "Analyzing community signals..."

2. **What We Found** 🔍
   - The type of stress detected (anxiety, burnout, etc.)
   - An intensity bar showing how severe it is
   - What people are actually saying

3. **Recommended Intervention** 💡
   - The therapeutic approach (CBT or DBT)
   - Visual style for the "Mindful Moment" video
   - Message approach to help

## What's Happening Behind the Scenes?

When you click a button:

1. **Monitor Agent** 🔍 analyzes the community content
   - Detects the type of stress (anxiety, burnout, isolation, etc.)
   - Calculates severity (0-100%)
   - Identifies the community context

2. **Strategy Agent** 🧠 creates a therapeutic plan
   - Maps to CBT or DBT framework
   - Selects appropriate techniques
   - Designs intervention guidelines

3. **Results Display** 📊 shows you everything visually
   - Emojis and colors for easy understanding
   - Progress bars for intensity
   - Clear, simple language

## No Technical Knowledge Needed!

- ✅ Just click buttons
- ✅ See visual results
- ✅ Understand what people are feeling
- ✅ See how we can help

## Need Help?

If nothing happens when you click:
1. Make sure you're at http://localhost:5001
2. Check that the terminal shows the server is running
3. Try refreshing the page (Cmd+R or Ctrl+R)
